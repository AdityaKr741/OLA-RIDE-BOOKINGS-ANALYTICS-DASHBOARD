# OLA-Ride-Booking-Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Uber Ride Booking Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The goal of the project is to understand ride-booking patterns from different perspectives such as ride volume, booking status, vehicle types, revenue, cancellations, and customer/driver ratings.

Instead of looking at the raw booking data directly, the dashboard organizes the information into five focused views, making it easier to explore the business performance and identify useful patterns.

---

##  Project Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- How does the number of rides change over time?
- What is the overall booking status distribution?
- Which vehicle types cover the highest ride distances?
- Which payment methods generate the most revenue?
- Who are the top customers based on total booking value?
- How is ride distance distributed throughout the day?
- Why are customers cancelling rides?
- Why are drivers cancelling rides?
- How do customer and driver ratings vary?

---

##  Dashboard Structure

The Power BI report is divided into **5 analytical pages**.

### 1.  Overall

The Overall page provides a quick view of the general ride-booking performance.

It includes:

- **Ride Volume Over Time**
- **Booking Status Breakdown**

This page helps understand overall booking activity and how rides are distributed across different booking statuses.

---

### 2.  Vehicle Type

This page focuses on the performance of different vehicle types.

It includes:

- **Top 5 Vehicle Types by Ride Distance**

This helps compare the distance covered by different vehicle categories and understand which vehicle types contribute significantly to overall ride activity.

---

### 3.  Revenue

The Revenue page focuses on the financial side of the ride-booking business.

It includes:

- **Revenue by Payment Method**
- **Top 5 Customers by Total Booking Value**
- **Ride Distance Distribution Per Day**

This view helps understand how customers pay, which customers contribute the most booking value, and how ride activity varies throughout the day.

---

### 4.  Cancellation

The Cancellation page analyzes cancelled rides from both customer and driver perspectives.

It includes:

- **Cancelled Ride Reasons – Customers**
- **Cancelled Ride Reasons – Drivers**

Separating customer and driver cancellations makes it easier to understand the different reasons behind unsuccessful bookings.

---

### 5.  Ratings

The Ratings page focuses on service quality and user feedback.

It includes:

- **Driver Ratings**
- **Customer Ratings**

This allows the performance of drivers and customer experience to be explored through rating patterns.
